brightframedesigns Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

brightframedesigns Shop Cofounder is a personal tool used only by its owner, Sohrab (shop: brightframedesigns), for the owner's own Etsy shop. It is not offered to the public.

What the tool does

With the owner's permission, it connects to the owner's own Pinterest account to create pins for the owner's own Etsy listings and to read the analytics (impressions, saves, clicks) of the pins it created.

Data

The tool does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person. Access tokens and pin records are stored only on the owner's own computer. The tool does not access other users' pins, boards or accounts.
